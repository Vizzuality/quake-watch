# Quake Watch

Earthquakes around the world, shown in terms of the places people care about.

## Language

**Earthquake**:
A seismic event reported by a seismological agency (EMSC by default, USGS on request), with a time, an Epicenter, a Depth and a Magnitude.
_Avoid_: Quake, tremor, event
_Spanish_: Terremoto

**Epicenter**:
The point on the Earth's surface directly above where the Earthquake started.
_Avoid_: Center, origin, location of the quake
_Spanish_: Epicentro

**Depth**:
How far below the Epicenter the rupture began, in km.
_Avoid_: Focus, hypocenter
_Spanish_: Profundidad

**Magnitude**:
The energy the Earthquake released. One number per Earthquake, the same everywhere.
_Avoid_: Strength, power, size, Intensity
_Spanish_: Magnitud

**Intensity**:
How strongly the shaking was felt at one particular place, on the Modified Mercalli scale (I–X). Varies by place for the same Earthquake.
_Avoid_: Magnitude, strength, force
_Spanish_: Intensidad

**Place of interest**:
A location the user cares about (their home, their town) against which an Earthquake's effect is explained.
_Avoid_: Location, marker, pin
_Spanish_: Lugar de interés

**Shaking wave**:
A ring that spreads from the Epicenter out to its Felt reach and fades as the estimated Intensity drops. Its fronts travel at real P and S wave speeds from the rupture start, Depth included, with each Earthquake's real seconds squeezed into the same few seconds on screen. The S front reaches the Felt reach as the ring ends.
_Avoid_: Ripple, shockwave
_Spanish_: Onda sísmica

**Isoseismal**:
A line on a USGS ShakeMap joining places with the same estimated Intensity.
_Avoid_: Contour, wave
_Spanish_: Isosista

**Felt reach**:
The distance from the Epicenter within which the estimated Intensity is at least II.
_Avoid_: Radius, range
_Spanish_: Alcance sentido

**Interval**:
How far back from now the globe shows Earthquakes, and the stretch the timeline replays: 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days. Always relative to when the page is opened.
_Avoid_: Period, range, Playback window
_Spanish_: Intervalo

**Likely felt**:
An Earthquake whose Felt reach at the surface is greater than zero, so people near the Epicenter would probably have noticed it.
_Avoid_: Felt (alone, which suggests felt reports), noticeable
_Spanish_: Probablemente sentido

**Depth class**:
A band of Depth: Shallow (0–70 km), Intermediate (70–300 km) or Deep (more than 300 km).
_Avoid_: Depth range, layer
_Spanish_: Clase de profundidad (Superficial, Intermedio, Profundo)

**Sequence**:
A group of Earthquakes close in place and time within the Interval. The largest is the Mainshock; each other one is a Foreshock, an Aftershock or a Doublet.
_Avoid_: Cluster, swarm, related quakes
_Spanish_: Secuencia

**Mainshock**:
The largest Earthquake of a Sequence.
_Avoid_: Main quake, largest event
_Spanish_: Terremoto principal

**Foreshock**:
An Earthquake of a Sequence that happened before its Mainshock.
_Avoid_: Precursor, warning quake
_Spanish_: Premonitor

**Aftershock**:
A smaller Earthquake of a Sequence that happened after its Mainshock.
_Avoid_: Replica, follow-up
_Spanish_: Réplica

**Doublet**:
An Earthquake of a Sequence that happened after its Mainshock with almost the same Magnitude, within 0.05.
_Avoid_: Twin, second mainshock
_Spanish_: Doblete

**Crust layer**:
One band of the ground under the Epicenter (water, ice, sediments, upper, middle or lower crust), with the Depth of its top and bottom.
_Avoid_: Stratum, level
_Spanish_: Capa de la corteza

**Moho**:
The boundary between the crust and the mantle under the Epicenter.
_Avoid_: Crust bottom, mantle top
_Spanish_: Moho

**Crustal block**:
A 3D cut of the ground around the Epicenter that shows its Crust layers and the Depth at which the Earthquake started.
_Avoid_: Cross-section, Earth slice
_Spanish_: Bloque de corteza
