# Quake Watch

Earthquakes around the world, shown in terms of the places people care about.

## Language

**Earthquake**:
A seismic event reported by a seismological agency (EMSC by default, USGS on request), with a time, an Epicenter, a Depth and a Magnitude.
_Avoid_: Quake, tremor, event
_Spanish_: Terremoto

**Epicenter**:
The point on the Earth's surface directly above where the Earthquake started.
_Avoid_: Center, origin, location of the quake
_Spanish_: Epicentro

**Depth**:
How far below the Epicenter the rupture began, in km.
_Avoid_: Focus, hypocenter
_Spanish_: Profundidad

**Magnitude**:
The energy the Earthquake released. One number per Earthquake, the same everywhere.
_Avoid_: Strength, power, size, Intensity
_Spanish_: Magnitud

**Intensity**:
How strongly the shaking was felt at one particular place, on the Modified Mercalli scale (I–X). Varies by place for the same Earthquake.
_Avoid_: Magnitude, strength, force
_Spanish_: Intensidad

**Place of interest**:
A location the user cares about (their home, their town) against which an Earthquake's effect is explained.
_Avoid_: Location, marker, pin
_Spanish_: Lugar de interés

**Shaking wave**:
A ring that spreads from the Epicenter out to its Felt reach and fades as the estimated Intensity drops. Its speed is stylised, but its reach is real.
_Avoid_: Ripple, shockwave
_Spanish_: Onda sísmica

**Felt reach**:
The distance from the Epicenter within which the estimated Intensity is at least II.
_Avoid_: Radius, range
_Spanish_: Alcance sentido

**Playback window**:
The stretch of the searched period that the map plays back, 24 hours or 7 days long.
_Avoid_: Range, zoom
_Spanish_: Ventana

**Crust layer**:
One band of the ground under the Epicenter (water, ice, sediments, upper, middle or lower crust), with the Depth of its top and bottom.
_Avoid_: Stratum, level
_Spanish_: Capa de la corteza

**Moho**:
The boundary between the crust and the mantle under the Epicenter.
_Avoid_: Crust bottom, mantle top
_Spanish_: Moho

**Crustal block**:
A 3D cut of the ground around the Epicenter that shows its Crust layers and the Depth at which the Earthquake started.
_Avoid_: Cross-section, Earth slice
_Spanish_: Bloque de corteza
